    About Us


    Welcome to the website of the Wearable Devices and Embodied Artificial Intelligence Research Lab (WEAI). WEAI brings together researchers from Jiangnan University, the Technical University of Madrid, Newcastle University, and the University of California, Irvine. We explore how artificial intelligence can be integrated into human–device interactions in real-world environments.
